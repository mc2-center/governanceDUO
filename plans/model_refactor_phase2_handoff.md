- Every step ends with `make validate-all` passing, and then
  `make artifact-drift-check`. Run both in the background: they take longer
  than the tool timeout. Commit each step separately. Never commit a red tree,
  and check exit codes directly, not the exit code of a pipe into `tail`.
- The old pipeline's SHACL and example RDF reorder their blank nodes on
  rebuild. If `git status` shows them modified after a run, `git checkout`
  them; don't commit them.
- Don't touch `docs/graph-design.md` or `requirements.txt`. Those are the
  user's own uncommitted edits.
- No pushing, tags or PRs.
- End each commit message with
  `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- Match the surrounding code: docstring-led modules, the `warn()` convention,
  no bare IRIs strings outside `graph_iris.py`.

## Steps

### 1. Derivation builder on the canonical graph

Rewrite `scripts/build_derivation_policy.py`. Keep `compute_control_labels`
and `compute_derivation_reviews` unchanged in logic.

- **Inputs:** `--graph` (repeatable; the canonical governance and provenance
  graphs), `--extra-graph` (repeatable; for sagebrain), and `--derivation-rules`
  (record-layer DerivationRule YAML, loaded as now). Drop
  `--access-requirement-dir`: the tier is now on the AR node.
- **An entity's ARs** are its `gov:requiresAR` edges plus those of every
  ancestor over `gov:parent+`.
- **An AR's tier** comes from its `gov:dataTier` concept, mapped from the
  concept IRI back to its code through the DataTier enum's `meaning`s. No tier
  means `Unclassified`. Ranks come from `GraphBuilder.rank()`; delete
  `DATA_TIER_RANK`.
- **Derivation edges:** run `projections/provenance.rq`, then add
  `rdfs:subPropertyOf* prov:wasDerivedFrom` properties (keep
  `derivation_properties`). Activity inputs are usages with
  `gov:wasExecuted false`, under the new namespace.
- **Output:** through `GraphBuilder`, written with `graph_rdf.to_rdf`.
  - A ControlLabel gets `id=graph_iris.control_label(subject)`, `subject`,
    `dataTier` (the code), `sourceAccessRequirements` and `computedFrom`.
  - A DerivationReview gets `id=graph_iris.derivation_review(activity)`, with
    status `Flagged`, the same notes text as today, and `inputLabels` for the
    labeled inputs only.
- **Fixture:** convert `linkml/examples/derivation_policy/fixture/` to
  the new model:
  - `governance.ttl` and `provenance.ttl` become canonical graph YAML
    (`GovernanceGraph`) with RDF generated by `graph_rdf.py`, or they stay
    hand-written Turtle in the new namespace that conforms to
    `shapes/governance.shacl.ttl`;
  - AR tiers go on the AR nodes, replacing the fixture's
    `access_requirements/`;
  - `sagebrain.ttl` keeps its `sagebrain:derived_from` subproperty.
- **Check:** rewrite `scripts/check_derivation_policy.py` with the same 11
  assertions and the review count of 4, in the new namespace. Concept IRIs
  replace literals, e.g. `gov:dataTier gov:UnclassifiedTier`. Replace the
  domain/range part with SHACL: validate the output merged with its inputs
  against `shapes/governance.shacl.ttl`.
- **Makefile:** `derivation-policy` builds from the canonical example graph
  (`linkml/examples/graph/rdf/governance_graph.ttl`), and `derivation-policy-check`
  depends on `graph-tbox`.

### 2. Projections (moved up from Phase 3)

- **`projections/authorizer_v1.rq`**, from
  `plans/model_refactor_spike/authorizer_v1.rq`. It builds the infra `fc6de51`
  contract in the old namespace `https://sagebionetworks.org/governance/`:
  - one `gov:AccessGrant` per (entity, direct agent), for every entity with a
    `gov:benefactor`;
  - `gov:permission` is the mode's `skos:notation` as an old-namespace IRI,
    plus `gov:ACCESS` wherever `DOWNLOAD` is granted;
  - `gov:bindingType` is `gov:Direct` when the entity is its own benefactor,
    otherwise `gov:Inferred`;
  - `gov:principal` is `gov:principal-<id>`, and `gov:source gov:Synapse`;
  - `gov:hasAccessRequirement` goes from each entity to every AR on it or an
    ancestor (`gov:parent*`), in old-namespace `gov:AR-<n>` form;
  - `gov:hasApproval` goes from the holder to the AR for each Approval with
    status `gov:Approved` whose `gov:expiresAt` is absent or later than an
    as-of time substituted in by the runner.
- **`projections/authorizer_v1_teams.rq`**, from the spike's teams file: the
  flattened team grants with `gov:viaTeam`, carrying the source grant's
  binding type. It runs only with `TEAMS=1`.
- **`scripts/project.py`** runs a projection over the given graphs plus the
  TBox (the modes' notations live there) and writes Turtle. It takes
  `--as-of`, an ISO datetime, and prints a count of `acl:agentClass`
  authorizations that `authorizer_v1` can't express.
- **Makefile:** `projections` writes
  `governance_graph_export/authorizer_v1.ttl` (plus `_teams.ttl` when
  `TEAMS=1`) from the canonical example graph, using
  `--as-of $(GOVERNANCE_GRAPH_AS_OF_ISO)`. That's the ISO form of today's
  `GOVERNANCE_GRAPH_AS_OF`, `2026-01-01T00:00:00Z`.
- **`scripts/check_infra_contract.py`** runs against
  `governance_graph_export/authorizer_v1.ttl`.
- **New `scripts/check_projections.py`:**
  - Golden diff against `tests/golden/governance_graph.ttl`, restricted to
    the authorizer-relevant triples (hasACL, AccessGrant and its four
    properties, hasAccessRequirement, hasApproval), compared after mapping
    IRIs. The allowed differences are `Inherited`→`Inferred`, extra
    hasAccessRequirement edges on the AR's own subject, and grant IRIs. Any
    other difference fails.
  - An expired approval produces no `hasApproval`; the same approval before
    its expiry does. This replaces `check_approval_expiry.py`.
  - A team member gets a grant only with the teams projection.
  - A descendant's grant is `Inferred`.
- **Retire** `approval-expiry-check` and `check_approval_expiry.py`.
