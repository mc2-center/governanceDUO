# Model refactor: one graph layer, standard vocabularies, derived projections

## Context

Three read-only research passes (2026-09-23) looked at sagebrain-model's
conventions, sagebrain-infra's authorizer and PR #54, and this repo's own seams.
Their findings, in short:

- **Two TBoxes describe one namespace.** 78 of the hand-written
  `governance_graph.owl.ttl`'s 84 `gov:` IRIs are also declared by the generated
  `governance_duo.owl.ttl`, several differently (Class vs NamedIndividual,
  `xsd:integer` vs `xsd:long`, different superclasses of `Team`/`User`). About 45 of
  the 139 commits since the hand TBox was created, and about 6 of `validate-all`'s
  14 checks, exist to keep the two TBoxes and three RDF emitters in agreement.
- **Graph terms have no single home.** `hasACL`, `hasAccessRequirement`,
  `hasApproval`, `affiliatedWith` and `statusModifiedOn` have no LinkML slot, while
  `ControlLabel` and `DerivationReview` are missing from the hand TBox. The builder
  mints its own IRIs even though `graph_iris.py` claims that job.
- **Vocabularies don't match sagebrain-model.** It models every value set as SKOS
  (a concept class, a ConceptScheme, `prefLabel`/`definition`/`inScheme`). Our
  `owl:oneOf` attempt hung pySHACL and was reverted (`b4b01ec`).
- **The authorizer reads explicit triples only.** Neptune does no reasoning. The
  authorizer (infra `fc6de51`) reads only an entity's own `gov:hasACL`, matches
  principals by id with no team expansion, accepts only `"Direct"`/`"Inferred"`
  binding types (so our `gov:Inherited` can never be permitted), and ignores
  approvals in the graph.
- **Fidelity gaps.** There is no team membership. PUBLIC and AUTHENTICATED_USERS
  grants authorize no one. AR concrete types are dropped by the sync. Approvals are
  filtered to "active at build time" instead of being recorded with status and
  expiry. Timestamps are epoch-ms `xsd:long` where the standard (and PR #54) is
  `xsd:dateTime`.

The user's decisions (2026-09-23):

1. Adopt PR #54's convention, **modeling under the new namespace while still
   emitting the old one until infra moves** (option 1b).
2. Rename binding type `Inherited` to `Inferred`.
3. Teams: membership edges are the canonical record. Flattened per-user grants are
   a separate, flagged, derived stopgap, each carrying `gov:viaTeam` and the
   binding type of its source grant.
4. Edit only this repo. Changes needed in sagebrain-infra and sagebrain-model are
   written down, not made.
5. Where a better standard convention exists, use it.

## Design principle

**The canonical graph records facts; everything an evaluator needs pre-computed is
a projection derived from it.** Inherited ACLs, flattened team grants, "active
approval" shortcuts, `gov:ACCESS` next to `DOWNLOAD` and the legacy namespace are all
derivations. They leave the canonical graph and move into versioned projections,
each generated by a SPARQL `CONSTRUCT` from the canonical graph and checked against
the consumer that reads it. This is what makes 1b clean: the canonical model can
follow the best conventions without breaking the live authorizer, because the
authorizer reads a projection pinned to its own contract.

## Conventions adopted

Where a W3C or community standard already covers a concept, the canonical graph
uses its terms directly rather than a `gov:` term mapped by `rdfs:subPropertyOf`.
Neptune doesn't infer, so a mapping axiom alone wouldn't make the standard term
queryable.

| Concern | Convention | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ACL entries | `acl:Authorization` with `acl:accessTo`, `acl:default`, `acl:agent`, `acl:agentGroup`, `acl:agentClass`, `acl:mode` | W3C/Solid Web Access Control, `http://www.w3.org/ns/auth/acl#` |
| Synapse permissions | Each `ACCESS_TYPE` value is an access mode, `rdfs:subClassOf acl:Access` (or `acl:Read`/`acl:Write`/`acl:Control` where it means that), which is WAC's own extension mechanism. The same IRI is also a `skos:Concept` in `gov:PermissionScheme`, with `skos:notation "DOWNLOAD"` (class/individual punning is legal in OWL 2 DL) | WAC; SKOS |
| Inheritance | The ACL is recorded once, on its benefactor, with `acl:accessTo` and `acl:default` (WAC's "applies to descendants without their own ACL", which is exactly Synapse's benefactor rule), plus `gov:benefactor` on each entity. Materialized per-entity grants are a projection | WAC |
| Teams | `vcard:Group` with `vcard:hasMember`, the group model WAC names | WAC, vCard |
| PUBLIC / AUTHENTICATED_USERS | `acl:agentClass foaf:Agent` / `acl:agentClass acl:AuthenticatedAgent` | WAC |
| Value sets | sagebrain's pattern: concept class `⊑ skos:Concept`, `skos:ConceptScheme`, `skos:prefLabel`, `skos:definition`, `skos:inScheme`, `skos:notation` for the exact Synapse or DUO code, `skos:exactMatch`/`closeMatch` for mappings | SKOS; sagebrain-model |
| Approvals | `gov:Approval` (PR #54's name) with `gov:heldBy`, `gov:satisfies`, `gov:status` (a concept), `gov:expiresAt`, documented as `skos:closeMatch` of GA4GH Passport `ControlledAccessGrants` (already referenced in `docs/drs-interop.md`) | PR #54; GA4GH Passports |
| Timestamps | `xsd:dateTime` everywhere | XSD; PR #54 |
| Data use | DUO term IRIs on the AR (`gov:dataUseCondition <obo:DUO_…>`); disease as a MONDO IRI (`gov:diseaseContext`), not a CURIE literal | DUO, MONDO |
| Provenance | PROV-O unchanged; stop asserting superclasses and SKOS labels on `prov:` classes ("not ours to give") | PROV-O; sagebrain-model |
| Term metadata | `rdfs:label`, `skos:definition`, `owl:versionInfo` on every `gov:` term; `skos:scopeNote` on reused terms | sagebrain-model |
| Shapes | Their own namespace (`…/governance/shapes#`), not the term namespace | sagebrain-model |
| Naming | Classes UpperCamel, properties lowerCamel, the W3C norm (PROV, WAC, DCAT, ODRL) and PR #54's. sagebrain uses snake_case for its own properties; that divergence is recorded, and both sides are consistent within their own namespace | W3C; PR #54 |

### Decisions this section needs you to confirm

These follow instruction 5, but they go beyond "PR #54's convention", so they are
flagged rather than assumed:

- **R1. Namespace:** `https://w3id.org/synapse/governance#`, not
  `https://sagebase.org/governance#`. It keeps PR #54's hash style and term names,
  but uses a persistent w3id IRI next to sagebrain's `https://w3id.org/synapse/sagebrain#`.
  A domain IRI breaks if the domain moves; w3id is the ontology-community standard
  for exactly that reason. Resolving it needs a w3id redirect entry (an outward PR
  to `perma-id/w3id.org`), which is written down, not filed. The alternative is
  PR #54's IRI as written.
- **R2. Instance IRIs sit beside the model, not inside the term namespace**
  (sagebrain's rule): `https://w3id.org/synapse/governance/ar/42`,
  `…/approval/<id>`, `…/authorization/<aclId>-<principalId>`.
- **R3. Principals use Synapse's own URLs**, the same way entities already use
  `syn:`: `https://www.synapse.org/Profile:<id>` for users and
  `https://www.synapse.org/Team:<id>` for teams. There is one IRI per real thing,
  and no `gov:principal-<n>` minting. The legacy projection still writes
  `gov:principal-<n>` for the authorizer's suffix match.
- **R4. WAC as the canonical ACL vocabulary**, as in the table above. The
  alternative keeps a `gov:AccessGrant` of our own. WAC is the only W3C-track RDF
  ACL model, and it already has the three things we lack: groups, public and
  authenticated classes, and inheritance.
- **R5. One IRI per AR.** The curated record and the graph node share the AR's IRI,
  which removes the `AccessRequirementReference` stub class and its `owl:sameAs`
  bridge. Record-only attributes are simply more triples on the same subject.
- **R6. Resource → AR edge:** `gov:requiresAR` (PR #54's name), with range
  `gov:AccessRequirement`. PR #54 aims it at a template; here
  `gov:AccessRequirementTemplate` becomes a subclass of `gov:AccessRequirement`,
  so both uses type-check.

## Target structure

```
linkml/
  graph/                  graph layer: owns every gov: term; imports no record module
    vocabularies.yaml     graph enums -> SKOS schemes (permission, binding type,
                          approval/submission status, AR concrete type, source, tier)
    access.yaml           Authorization, Group membership, benefactor
    requirements.yaml     AccessRequirement(+Template), Condition, Approval, Submission
    provenance.yaml       Activity/Usage graph terms, ControlLabel, DerivationReview
  record/                 record layer (governanceduo:): curation records; may import graph/
  governance_duo.linkml.yaml   umbrella, unchanged role
shapes/
  governance.owl.ttl      the one graph TBox, generated (replaces both TBoxes)
  governance.shacl.ttl    the one graph shape set, generated
  record.owl.ttl / record.shacl.ttl   record validation only; not imported by sagebrain
projections/
  authorizer_v1.rq        CONSTRUCT: canonical -> infra fc6de51 contract
  authorizer_v1_teams.rq  CONSTRUCT: flattened team grants (flag-gated)
scripts/
  graph_iris.py           the only IRI minter
  build_owl.py            + emit SKOS schemes for enums marked as vocabularies
  build_graph.py          one emitter: builds LinkML graph objects, dumps with RDFLibDumper
  project.py              runs projections; writes governance_graph_export/<name>.ttl
docs/downstream_changes.md    change list for sagebrain-infra and sagebrain-model
```

## Phases

Each phase ends green (`make validate-all`, `artifact-drift-check`,
`sagebrain-contract-check`) and is committed separately.

### Phase 0: golden baseline and spike

- Freeze today's `governance_graph_export/` and the sync fixture output as
  `tests/golden/` baselines.
- **Spike (throwaway), to prove the risky parts before committing to them:**
  - WAC modes punned as class and SKOS concept pass the OWL 2 DL profile.
  - pySHACL validation of a SKOS-typed vocabulary stays at seconds.
  - An owlgen extension emits a ConceptScheme from an enum marked with
    `annotations: {vocabulary: true}`.
  - `RDFLibDumper` writes a principal whose identifier slot holds the full
    `https://www.synapse.org/Profile:<id>` IRI. This removes the blocker that
    stopped the emitter consolidation in `governance_consolidation_and_drs_interop`.
  - If any of these fails, stop and bring the finding back before continuing.

### Phase 1: graph-layer schema and SKOS vocabularies

- Create `linkml/graph/`. Every graph term gets a slot or class there, including
  the five hand-TBox-only predicates, and `ControlLabel`/`DerivationReview` move in.
  Default prefix is `gov:` under the new namespace (R1). The linkml-lint
  canonical-prefix collision that forced `sagegov:` gets a lint exception rather
  than a second prefix.
- Graph enums become SKOS vocabularies:
  - `BindingType`: `Direct`, `Inferred` (decision 2).
  - Approval status and submission state share `gov:Approved`, one concept in both
    schemes. SKOS allows this, which settles the shared-`gov:APPROVED` open item.
  - `AccessRequirementConcreteType`: TermsOfUse, SelfSign, ManagedACT, Lock.
  - Permissions: one concept per Synapse `ACCESS_TYPE`, `skos:notation` = the
    Synapse string, `rdfs:subClassOf` the matching WAC mode. `GrantPermissionEnum`
    and its `enum-sync-check` guard are deleted: `ACCESS` stops being a stored
    value and is written only by the projection.
  - DataTier is a scheme whose ordering sits on the concepts, so
    `DATA_TIER_RANK` in `build_derivation_policy.py` reads it instead of hard-coding it.
- `build_owl.py` emits the ConceptScheme, concept typing and SKOS metadata. SHACL
  checks vocabulary slots with `sh:in` generated from scheme membership plus
  `sh:nodeKind sh:IRI`, so the ABox doesn't need to restate concept types.
- Generated OWL and SHACL are the only graph TBox and shape set. Delete
  `governance_graph.owl.ttl`, `governance_graph.shacl.ttl` and
  `provenance_layer.shacl.ttl` (merged in). The record layer's OWL stops asserting
  on `prov:` classes.

### Phase 2: one IRI minter, one emitter

- `graph_iris.py` mints every IRI (R2, R3, R5). No other module builds IRI strings.
- `build_graph.py` replaces `build_governance_graph.py`. It builds LinkML graph-layer
  objects and dumps them with `RDFLibDumper`. `sync_governance_graph.py` and
  `build_derivation_policy.py` build the same objects instead of writing triples.
- Fixes that fall out of the new model:
  - The ACL is written once per benefactor with `acl:default`, plus
    `gov:benefactor` per entity. There's no per-entity materialization here.
  - Team, PUBLIC and AUTHENTICATED_USERS principals use `acl:agentGroup` /
    `acl:agentClass`.
  - The sync fetches team membership (`GET /team/{id}/member`, paged) for every team
    that appears on an ACL and writes `vcard:hasMember` edges. Group membership in
    the graph is a data-exposure decision; the graph-design doc gains a note on it.
  - The sync keeps the AR's `concreteType` and `accessType`, and only fetches
    submissions for managed ARs.
  - Every approval is recorded with status, version and expiry, including expired
    and revoked ones; nothing is filtered by `--as-of`. `requirementVersion` is
    compared with the AR's current version by a check.
  - Every timestamp is written as `xsd:dateTime`.
  - DUO terms and MONDO contexts are written as IRIs.
- Retire checks that only guarded the seam: `enum-sync-check`, the merged-TBox part
  of `owl-profile`, and the TBox-agreement assertions in `domain-range-check`,
  `sync-governance-check` and `derivation-policy-check`. Each one's still-useful
  assertions move to `check_graph.py`, a single check over the one TBox.

### Phase 3: projections

- `authorizer_v1.rq` builds the fc6de51 contract from the canonical graph:
  - the old namespace `https://sagebionetworks.org/governance/`;
  - one `gov:AccessGrant` per entity, principal and permission, with
    `gov:principal gov:principal-<id>`, `gov:permission` = the concept's notation as
    a `gov:` IRI, plus `gov:ACCESS` wherever `DOWNLOAD` is granted (D8);
  - `gov:bindingType gov:Direct` on the benefactor and `gov:Inferred` on the
    materialized descendants;
  - `gov:hasAccessRequirement` from `gov:requiresAR`.
- `authorizer_v1_teams.rq` (only with `make projections TEAMS=1`) expands each
  `acl:agentGroup` grant into per-member grants that carry `gov:viaTeam` and copy
  the source grant's binding type (decision 3).
  - `acl:agentClass` grants (PUBLIC, AUTHENTICATED_USERS) can't be flattened. The
    projection emits nothing for them and the build prints a count, so the gap is
    visible rather than silent.
- `check_infra_contract.py` runs the pinned `authorize_query.rq` against the
  projection. A golden test diffs the projection against the Phase 0 baseline.
  The only differences allowed are `Inherited` → `Inferred`, the added team grants,
  and approvals that the old builder filtered out.
- The release carries the canonical graph plus the projections, each versioned.

### Phase 4: sagebrain-model alignment (this repo only)

- Every `gov:` term gets `rdfs:label`, `skos:definition` and `owl:versionInfo`.
  Shapes move to the shapes namespace. `gov:SynapseEntity` gets its own
  `skos:closeMatch prov:Entity`, which sagebrain currently adds on our behalf.
- `check_sagebrain_contract.py` is rewritten against the single TBox. It
  reproduces what sagebrain's `import.sh` will need: one source file, no
  `BaseEntity` rooting trick.
- Prepare release `v0.2.0`. The tag is **not** pushed; that is outward-facing.

### Phase 5: docs and change lists

- Rewrite `docs/graph-design.md` and `docs/knowledge-graph.md` for the new model,
  and delete the stale headers the inventory found.
- `docs/downstream_changes.md` lists the changes for other repos, none of them made:
  - **sagebrain-infra:**
    - read the new namespace, or keep reading `authorizer_v1`;
    - join `acl:agentGroup` through `vcard:hasMember` and handle `acl:agentClass`,
      then drop the teams projection;
    - allow inferred-only grants (intersection mode denies them, and most files
      inherit their ACL);
    - evaluate `gov:Approval` from the graph, and stop failing open when the caller
      omits approvals;
    - select the latest snapshot, so revocation takes effect;
    - make the rewriter use IRIs;
    - fix the `.rq` header's org name.
  - **sagebrain-model:** re-point `import.sh` at the single TBox, adopt the new
    namespace, and drop its own `SynapseEntity` closeMatch.
  - **w3id:** a redirect entry for R1.

## Out of scope

- Query-time enforcement, DUO/MONDO matching logic, and what revoking an AR means
  for derived content. These are policy-layer questions (graph-design §7).
- Live-Synapse verification of the sync. It still runs against fixtures, as before.

## Risks

- **owlgen:** SKOS emission and WAC punning are generator work. The Phase 0 spike
  gates them.
- **Scale of membership edges:** large teams add many edges. They are still linear
  in team size, unlike flattening, which is entities × members and is flag-gated.
- **Breaking downstream consumers:** everything in the canonical graph changes IRI.
  Only `authorizer_v1` promises continuity. sagebrain-model's pinned import keeps
  working until its pin is bumped.

## Verification

- Every phase: `make validate-all`, `make artifact-drift-check` and
  `make sagebrain-contract-check` pass.
- `check_infra_contract.py` passes against `authorizer_v1`, and the golden diff
  shows only the allowed differences.
- New checks fail when their bug is put back:
  - a team member is authorized only with `TEAMS=1`;
  - a descendant's grant is `Inferred`;
  - an expired approval stays in the canonical graph but out of the projection's
    shortcut;
  - a vocabulary value outside its scheme fails SHACL.
- OWL 2 DL on the single TBox plus all ABoxes.
- pySHACL timings are recorded in the report.
